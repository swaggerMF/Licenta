# TeD

**Your Type 1 bear analyst**

TeD aims to help people with type 1 diabetes make sense of their glucose history. It brings CareLink data into a simple calendar, where users can explore a month at a glance or open a day to follow their glucose readings in detail.

The goal is to make it easier to notice recurring patterns, periods of high or low glucose, and gaps in sensor readings. These views should help users better understand their data and prepare for conversations with their care team.

With TeD the bear as a companion, reviewing glucose data should feel clearer and more approachable.
